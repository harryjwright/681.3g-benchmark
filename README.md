# Case Report: Harry Wright v Nottingham Trent University (NTU)

## 📋 Overview
This repository contains the public case file regarding the dispute between **Harry Wright** (Student) and **Nottingham Trent University (NTU)** regarding the **MSc Smart Agriculture** programme (2024–2026).

It documents allegations of **Intellectual Property (IP) theft**, **procedural misconduct**, **data breaches**, and **financial conflict of interest** between the University and its commercial partner, **Light Science Technologies Holdings PLC (LST.L)**.

---

## ⚖️ Key Allegations

- **IP Theft & Commercial Conflict:** The University secured a government tender (approx. £548,126) with LST.L while the Student's research was being utilised for the tender's "live software telemetry" without attribution or consent.

- **Disciplinary Misconduct:** The Student was issued a Formal Warning following a disciplinary process where key evidence (emails) was allegedly forged or metadata-manipulated.

- **Data Protection Breaches:** Subject Access Requests (SAR) were refused or manipulated (Ref: ICO Complaints), including the inclusion of "OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE" documents in generic disclosures.

- **Material Disadvantage:** The Student withdrew from the MSc programme due to the cumulative stress of the investigation and resource restrictions, resulting in loss of qualification.
